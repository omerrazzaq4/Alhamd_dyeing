🏭 Al-Hamd Dyeing - Combined Bill Tools

Select a tool below to manage your business operations

📋 Wood Bill Sheet: Manage and track wood-related orders and bills
🎨 Dyes Bill Sheet: Track dye orders with price, paid amounts, and outstanding balances
💰 Cashbook Analyzer: Analyze party-wise cashbook entries with debit/credit summaries

📊 Party Reconciliation: View merged ledger with bills and payments for each party
